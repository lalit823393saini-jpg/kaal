KAALTime bends for one blade.

You can slow time whenever you want, for as long as you want. Everyone else keeps living at normal speed. Get within 10 metres of them and they come for you. Earn coins in levels, Boss Rush, Blitz and Survival, then spend them in the Dojo on swords, breathing styles and abilities.

Controls

AD move   W/Space jump   J/click slash   K/right-click block   L dash
Shift hold to slow time   E toggle slow time   Esc pause
1–5 breathing forms (the Fourth and Fifth Forms unlock once every Body upgrade is maxed)   Q throw kunai   R shadow clone
L then J Dash Slash: dash, then slash to cut up to 6 enemies within 10 m. 10 s cooldown.
H switch between your sword and your fists (after buying Iron Fists in the Dojo).
G fire your gun (Dojo > Firearms). Guns reload on their own when empty. The Hand Cannon fires while you hold G.
F throw your Divya Astra (Dojo > Divya Astra). It is not a sword and needs no breath: it sits in its own slot beside your sword.
Wings of Kaal (Dojo > Abilities): hold W/Jump in the air to fly. It drains breath.
The first time you use any breathing form, the fight slows down and a card explains it. Tap Skip, or any slash or jump, to carry on.
V get on or off your bike or car in Zombie Outbreak. Ram zombies at speed, L for a boost, W hops the bike. The car takes most of the damage for you.
With fists up: J punch   U kick   S crouch. Hold a direction while you hit for a different move — see Fist moves below for the full list.
Your stickman turns to face the nearest enemy within 10 m on his own. Hold a direction while slashing to aim the other way.

Block right before a hit lands to parry and stun the enemy. Red-glowing swings from brutes can't be blocked, only parried or dodged.
Breath fills when you land hits, parry, and slowly over time. Forms cost 30% less breath while time is slowed.
Levels: enemies drop coins, walk near them to collect. Free mode: coins go straight into your wallet on every kill.
Modes: Boss Rush is the four bosses against the clock, Blitz is 90 seconds of endless enemies where every kill adds a second, and Nightmare (on the level screen) replays a beaten level with much tougher enemies for double coins.
New in the Dojo: Reaper's Scythe, Storm Fang, Serpent Breathing (poison), and abilities like Phantom Dash, Last Stand, Time Thief and Third Hand (a third ability slot).

Your save code holds all your coins, weapons, astras and progress. Copy it here, then paste it into the other copy of the game (claude.ai or GitHub) and press Load.
